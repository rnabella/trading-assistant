Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Trading assistant is a private, personal tool used only by its two owners. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it uses

  • Gmail, read-only: it searches the owner's inbox only for brokerage account statement emails and downloads those statements. It does not read, change or delete any other email.
  • Gmail, send only: it sends alert emails from the owner's account to the two owners. It cannot read mail with this permission.

Where the data goes

Statements and alerts are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The only emails sent are the alerts to the two owners.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

An owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored statements can be deleted from the owner's computer at any time.
